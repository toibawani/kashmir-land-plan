Catalogue review sheet: Reasi

Jammu hills and mid-altitude valleys, intermediate. The transition belt between the plains and the high mountains. Climate changes quickly with height, so villages at different elevations in the same district can have quite different growing conditions. Much of the farming is rainfed.

2Fodder crop or managed pasture

Rainfed land here often suits fodder better than a demanding cash crop, and livestock are usually kept alongside the farming.

Main risk: Low cash return if there is no livestock to feed

3Improve what you already grow, or add an intercrop

Maize-wheat rotations on rainfed land leave gaps that a well-chosen second crop or mix can use.

Main risk: Changing something without advice can reduce the harvest you already have

4Kitchen garden and mixed vegetables

Something can be grown through most of the year, but the crop changes with height and the summer rains bring more disease pressure.

Main risk: Water running short at flowering and fruiting

☐☐
5Protected cultivation: polyhouse, mushroom or nursery

The cooler conditions help some crops, but access to the site and a reliable water supply need checking before spending anything.

Main risk: Disease or contamination causing a total loss while you are still learning

☐☐
6Sub-tropical fruit trees

This only works on the lower, warmer ground. The upper parts of the district are too cold for most sub-tropical fruit except in sheltered spots.

Main risk: Winter frost in the colder pockets

☐☐
7Temperate fruit and nut trees

Fruit is grown in the upper parts of these districts, so tree crops only make sense on ground that is high and cool enough for the species you pick.

Main risk: Frost or hail at flowering, which can cost a whole crop

How to reply

  1. Write your name, designation, office and the date at the top.
  2. Tick right or wrong beside each activity. If it is wrong, write why, and what should replace it.
  3. Mark any season, risk or local note that is misleading, even if the activity itself is fine.
  4. Hand the sheet back, or send a photo of it. Nothing here needs a computer.

The app never prints an amount, deadline, eligibility rule or yield promise. If a figure comes up, it is recorded as a question for the office, not as advice on screen.
