1Beekeeping as an allied activity

Orchards and oilseed crops around the valley give a forage season, though it is short and the colonies need managing for winter.

Main risk: Colony loss from disease, mites, or spray drifting from nearby fields

2Fodder crop or managed pasture

Fodder is already part of the cropping system in the valley, often grown after paddy, so the practice and the seed are both familiar locally.

Main risk: Low cash return if there is no livestock to feed

☐☐☐
3Improve what you already grow, or add an intercrop

Rice followed by mustard or oats is an established pattern in the valley, so a second crop along those lines is a known route rather than an experiment.

Main risk: Changing something without advice can reduce the harvest you already have

☐☐☐
4Kitchen garden and mixed vegetables

Vegetables are already grown across the valley, mostly for household use. The growing season is short, so timing matters more than the size of the plot.

Main risk: Water running short at flowering and fruiting

☐☐☐
5Protected cultivation: polyhouse, mushroom or nursery

Cover extends the short valley season at both ends, which is where much of the advantage comes from here.

Main risk: Disease or contamination causing a total loss while you are still learning

☐☐☐
6Temperate fruit and nut trees

Temperate fruit and nuts are the established system here, so planting material, advice and buyers are usually easier to find than for an unfamiliar crop.

Main risk: Frost or hail at flowering, which can cost a whole crop

How to reply

  1. Write your name, designation, office, the date and your signature at the top.
  2. Tick right, wrong or not sure beside each activity.
  3. Where it is wrong, write why, and what should replace it, in the last column.
  4. Mark a misleading season or risk note even when the activity itself is fine.
  5. Hand the sheet back, or send a photo of it. Nothing here needs a computer.

A line with no tick stays unreviewed, and the app keeps saying so. The app never prints an amount, deadline, eligibility rule or yield promise; if a figure comes up, it is recorded as a question for the office, not as advice on screen.

For the maintainer, not the officer: the prefilled rows are in Anantnag.csv, and a filled sheet imports with node tools/import-review.js Anantnag.csv. Prose corrections are applied to js/catalogue.js by hand before npm test.
