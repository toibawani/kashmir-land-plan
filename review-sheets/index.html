Catalogue review pack

For an agriculture or horticulture officer. One sheet per district, 8 activities in the app's list. There is nothing to install and nothing to edit: tick the boxes on paper and hand the sheet back.

What is being asked

Farmers use the app to turn a few details about an unused plot into questions to bring to an office. The suggestions it shows come from a short list of activities. Is anything on it wrong for your district? An option that does not make sense for the area, a month that is off for local practice, a reason that misleads, or something missing that farmers here are usually advised to consider. You do not need to approve anything. Crossing out a wrong line is the most useful thing you can do.

How to reply

  • Tick right, wrong or not sure beside each activity on your district sheet.
  • Write your name, designation, office, the date and your signature at the top.
  • Mark a misleading season or risk note even when the activity itself is fine.
  • Hand it back, or send a photo. Nothing here needs a computer.

What happens after

You never touch the app. The maintainer fixes or removes what you marked wrong, and records that you checked it: your name, designation, office and the date. Every screen then changes from "Not yet reviewed by an agriculture officer" to "Reviewed by [your name], [designation] on [date]". A row you marked not sure stays unreviewed. The line asking every farmer to confirm locally before acting does not go away, before or after your review.

5 of these districts span more than one kind of ground. The sheet lists the activities the app assumes for the district's usual ground, and says so at the top. If you serve a different part of the district, write which ground you are thinking of: that answer is as useful as a correction.

Printing

Take one district sheet, or print them all at once: all districts in one document (20 sheets, A4). Each district sheet also prints on its own. A prefilled CSV sits beside each sheet for the maintainer to type the answers into afterwards.

Kashmir valley, temperate

roughly 1,500 to 2,500 m, rising quickly on the side slopes. Cool temperate valley floor and side valleys. Farming here is mostly horticulture based, with paddy on the lower land and temperate fruit and nuts on the slopes and uplands. Winters are cold and the growing season is short.

Jammu hills and mid-altitude valleys, intermediate

roughly 700 to 2,500 m. The transition belt between the plains and the high mountains. Climate changes quickly with height, so villages at different elevations in the same district can have quite different growing conditions. Much of the farming is rainfed.

  • Doda (suggested ground: Mid-altitude hills, 7 activities)
  • Kishtwar (suggested ground: Mid-altitude hills, 8 activities)
  • Poonch (suggested ground: Mid-altitude hills, 7 activities)

  • Reasi (suggested ground: Mid-altitude hills, 7 activities)

Jammu plains and outer foothills, sub-tropical

roughly 250 to 700 m. Hot summers, a stronger monsoon and mild winters. Where water is available this is the most intensively cropped part of the region, and rainfed land carries maize and wheat.

  • Jammu (suggested ground: Plains and foothills, 6 activities)
  • Kathua (suggested ground: Plains and foothills, 6 activities)
  • Samba (suggested ground: Plains and foothills, 6 activities)
  • Udhampur (suggested ground: Plains and foothills, 6 activities)

For the maintainer, not the officer. This pack is sheet version v1.2.3 · catalogue d20337e. It is printed in each page footer and in every prefilled CSV, and the importer refuses a file printed from a different version. Regenerate this pack after any catalogue change with npm run review-sheets; the build fails if the pack does not match the catalogue. A filled sheet's CSV has the columns district, entry_id, entry_text, verdict, correction, reviewer_name, office, date, sheet_version; import it with node tools/import-review.js <file.csv>, and add --dry-run first to see exactly what would be recorded without writing anything. Apply prose corrections to js/catalogue.js by hand. Never mark anything reviewed without a real reply, a real name and a real date.
