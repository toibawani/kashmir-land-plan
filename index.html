My Land, My Plan

My Land, My Plan helps a farmer turn basic details about an underused plot into practical options and clear next steps for checking local support.

No account needed. Your details stay on this device only.

What you enter

District, village, plot size, water access, terrain, and the main constraint you face. Soil test is optional.

What you get

Two or three activity options to explore, a simple comparison, a next-step checklist, and pointers to official support channels.

Important

This is a hackathon prototype. Recommendations are illustrative. Always confirm with your local agriculture office or Kisan Khidmat Ghar.

Practical checklist

    Scheme and support pathway

    The following are general pointers only. This prototype does not verify eligibility, subsidy amounts, deadlines, or documents. Confirm current details with the official portal or local agriculture office.

    • Kisan Khidmat Ghar / Agriculture office

      Ask about soil testing, crop advisories, and any active schemes for your district. Take your land details and any soil report with you.

      Confirm current details with the official portal or local agriculture office.

    • Soil Health / testing services

      If you do not have a recent test, request information on how to get one through the local system.

      Confirm current details with the official portal or local agriculture office.

    • Central and UT agriculture schemes

      Schemes related to irrigation, horticulture, livestock, or farm mechanisation may exist. Rules and availability change by year and location.

      Confirm current details with the official portal or local agriculture office.

    • Market and cooperative support

      Ask about local mandis, FPOs, or cooperative channels if market access is a concern.

      Confirm current details with the official portal or local agriculture office.

    About and disclaimer

    My Land, My Plan is a hackathon prototype. It is not an official government service of the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir or any department.

    Product idea: My Land, My Plan helps a farmer turn basic details about an underused plot into practical options and clear next steps for checking local support.

    What this app does

    • Collects basic, non-identifying plot details you choose to enter
    • Shows illustrative activity options using simple sample logic
    • Provides a comparison view and a practical next-step checklist
    • Points you toward official channels without inventing scheme rules or amounts

    What this app does not do

    • Does not verify land ownership or records
    • Does not guarantee crop suitability or yield
    • Does not approve subsidies or determine scheme eligibility
    • Does not collect personal, financial, or land-identification data beyond what you type for this plan

    Data and privacy

    Draft plans can be saved in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded in this prototype. Clear your browser data to remove saved drafts.

    Sample data

    Recommendations and activity descriptions are marked as sample or illustrative. They are not based on real-time agronomic models or official databases. Always confirm crop choices, costs, water requirements, and current scheme rules with the relevant agriculture office or Kisan Khidmat Ghar.
