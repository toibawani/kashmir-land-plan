What this app is

My Land, My Plan is a free prototype that helps a farmer prepare questions and options for a visit to the agriculture office. It is run by an individual maintainer. It is not a company, and it is not a service of the Government of Jammu and Kashmir, the Government of India, or any department.

What it does not promise

  • It does not approve anything, and it cannot make you eligible for any scheme.
  • It does not assess your plot, verify your land records, or confirm what you typed.
  • It does not recommend a crop, predict a yield, or give a cost, income or water figure.
  • It does not give a field-level weather forecast, a frost warning or an irrigation schedule.
  • It does not replace an agriculture officer, a patwari, a soil test, or a lawyer.

Every option and question in the app is a starting point for a conversation. Nothing in it has been signed off by an agriculture officer, and that status is shown on screen. Confirm current rules, costs and suitability with your local agriculture office or Kisan Khidmat Ghar before you act or spend anything.
