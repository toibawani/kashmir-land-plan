Privacy Policy

This page says plainly what this app keeps, what leaves your device, and how to remove it. It describes the app as it works today. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Who runs this

My Land, My Plan is a personal, unfunded prototype. It is not a company, not a registered body, and not a government service. There is no office, no postal address and no phone number to give. The only contact is the project's public issue tracker: github.com/toibawani/kashmir-land-plan/issues.

There are no accounts

You do not sign in, register, or give a name, phone number or email to use this app. There is no login, no password, and no user profile of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The app uses your browser's own local storage, on the device you are holding. Nothing here is uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with another person by the app.

  • Your draft: the district, altitude zone, village name, plot size, water access, terrain, soil-test answer and main constraint you type into the form.
  • Saved plans: the same answers, plus the options that were shown, which options you marked, and which questions you ticked off at the office.
  • Your language choice, so the app opens in the language you picked.
  • A feedback record, only if you open the optional field-test page and save your answers there.
  • Photos you choose to take, kept in this browser's photo storage (IndexedDB), attached to the plan they were taken for. Each photo is redrawn smaller and re-saved as a new JPEG before it is stored, which drops the location and other camera metadata the original file carried.
  • A note beside a photo, if you type one. It is stored with that photo in the same place, on this device only, and it is not shared or uploaded either.

These values are stored as plain text in your browser's storage. Anyone who can use this phone and open this browser can read them. The app does not encrypt them, because a key kept beside the data would add ceremony without a real boundary.

What leaves your device

One thing, explained below: a weather request when you ask for the forecast. The app uses the fonts your device already has, so opening it contacts no other service. The land plan itself, the options, the visit card and the checklist never leave the device.

  • Weather. When you press the button to get a forecast, the district name or nearby town you chose is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) so it can return a forecast. Your village name is never sent. Like any web request, it also carries your device's IP address to that service. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.
  • Typefaces. None. The app used to load two fonts from Google Fonts on the first visit, which told Google your IP address and which page you opened. It now uses the fonts already on your device, so that request no longer happens.
  • Hosting. The app is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the request for the page and its files.

What this app does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party measurement.
  • No cookies. The app stores data in local storage, which is not sent with web requests.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no email addresses.
  • No server-side copy of your plans, your village, or your photos.
  • No sharing of your data with any other person through the app.
  • No photo analysis. A photo is never sent anywhere, not even to a service: it is resized and stored on this device, and that is all.
  • The visit card has a photo description section with a switch. In this build no description service is connected, so nothing is sent: the switch only remembers your answer on this device.

Your control over the data

  • Start over in the form removes the saved draft.
  • Delete on a saved plan removes that plan and its photos from this device.
  • Delete photo under a photo on the visit card removes just that photo.
  • Delete everything on this device, on the "On this device" screen, removes every plan, the draft, the language choice, the field-test answers and every photo.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything the app has stored, including plans and the language choice. That is also the way to remove it if you lose the phone or give it away.

Because there is no server copy, deleting on the device is final. There is nothing left for anyone to delete afterwards, and nothing for the project to recover for you.

Children

The app is written for farmers preparing an office visit. It collects no age, identity or contact information from anyone, so it holds nothing specific to a child.

Changes to this page

If a future version sends anything new off the device, this page will be updated in the same change and the date above will move. The date is the only version marker: there is no mailing list to notify.

Questions or a concern

Open an issue at github.com/toibawani/kashmir-land-plan/issues. Please do not put a real farmer's name, village or phone number in a public issue.

This policy is written to match what the app actually does, not as a legal instrument. It has not been checked by a lawyer.
